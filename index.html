THE PROBLEM

You finished the book.
How much do you still remember?

People spend hours reading nonfiction and understanding ideas in the moment. Weeks later, much of that knowledge is gone.

Rereading and highlighting may feel productive, but it’s not enough to build strong, lasting neural connections.

Bookreadle makes remembering what you read easier.

WHY QUIZZING WORKS

The quiz isn’t just a test.
It’s part of the learning.

It feels counterintuitive, but trying to retrieve an idea strengthens the path back to it. The retrieval-practice principles in Make It Stick show why low-stakes self-testing can produce more durable learning than simply rereading.
